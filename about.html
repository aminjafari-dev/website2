About Byto.tech GmbH

Byto.tech GmbH is a software studio that designs and ships mobile applications, websites, and AI automation, then reviews that work for security before it is treated as finished. The company is the legal entity behind the Byto.tech site. It was entered in the commercial register at the Amtsgericht Duisburg on 13 April 2026 under HRB 40364. The registered office is Großenbaumer Str. 143a, 45481 Mülheim an der Ruhr, Germany. A second office is at Leningradyan 4 in Yerevan, Armenia. Ahmad Jafari is the managing director named in the public register. The studio works with teams that already know the product they want and need engineering and a security review around it, rather than a slide deck about AI. The public pages describe that company and those services. They do not stand in for a customer list.

What the studio ships

The three services are mobile applications, websites, and AI automation. Each page states what is built and what a security review covers. The work page shows example product surfaces: screens and layouts that illustrate the kind of interface the studio designs. Those examples are not named client engagements, and they do not include a customer name or a measured result. When a real engagement is finished, the client decides what can be published. Until then, the public site describes the company, the services, and the offices, and it does not invent proof. Questions that repeat often are collected on the FAQ, in the same words the page shows to people and to structured-data readers. Each service also has its own URL, so a citation can point at mobile, websites, or AI automation without quoting the whole homepage.
